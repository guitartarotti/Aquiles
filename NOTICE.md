# Aquiles: Historical Attribution

Aquiles is an independent market-intelligence and quantitative-research platform.

Parts of its multi-agent simulation foundation were originally derived from the open-source [MiroFish project](https://github.com/666ghj/MiroFish). That upstream work was distributed under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3. Aquiles retains the same AGPL-3.0 license; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The current project has since been substantially modified with its own market-data collectors, quantitative models, regulatory-data pipelines, persistence, authentication, service orchestration and user interface. References to the upstream project are historical attribution only and do not indicate sponsorship, endorsement or an ongoing product identity.

The simulation engine also uses components from the [CAMEL-AI OASIS](https://github.com/camel-ai/oasis) ecosystem under their applicable licenses.
